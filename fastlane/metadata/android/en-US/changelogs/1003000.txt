WizeStream 1.3.0

- Added configurable bottom-navigation labels.
- Added optional two-finger swipe playback-speed control.
- Improved YouTube Music and Movies/Shows Trending charts.
- Improved recovery from YouTube DNS failures and SABR redirects.
- Fixed player gestures changing direction during a swipe.
- Changed the default playback speed to 1.0x for new installations.
- Fixed Nightly release retention.
- Reduced release APK size; official APKs now support ARM64 and ARMv7.
