The basics

What is WizeStream?+

An independent, NewPipe-based Android streaming client with a Material 3-oriented interface, flexible playback, local library tools and support for multiple sources.

Is this official NewPipe?+

No. WizeStream is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by TeamNewPipe or NewPipe e.V. It preserves upstream attribution and open-source licensing.

Do I need a Google or WizeStream account?+

Normal account-free browsing and playback do not require either. Your subscriptions and local playlists belong to the app’s library rather than a Google account.

What Android version do I need?+

Android 6.0 or newer. Use the normal ARM APK for ARM64/ARMv7 devices and the separate x86_64 APK for compatible Intel/AMD Android environments.

Does it need Google Play Services?+

WizeStream’s normal account-free functions do not require Google Play Services. Optional integrations can depend on Android features or separately installed applications.

Installation

Where should I download it?+

Use the download page for GitHub releases, IzzyOnDroid and other project links. Keep the same update source whenever possible.

Why does the app ID still say newpipematerial?+

The stable identity is retained so existing NewPipe Material installations can update to WizeStream without losing their app data.

Can stable, nightly and official NewPipe coexist?+

They use different application identities. Stable WizeStream, its separate nightly build and official NewPipe can be installed alongside one another.

Is the x86_64 APK a native Linux app?+

No. It is an Android APK for compatible environments such as Waydroid and Android-x86. The separately linked Desktop project has its own implementation.

Why does a guide mention a setting I cannot see?+

The handbook describes current development code. Some merged changes need a nightly build or a future stable release. Check release availability.

Playback

Can I listen with the screen off?+

Use supported background audio. Listen mode also continues the current item as audio while retaining its queue and position.

Does the visualizer record my microphone?+

No. The player visualizer uses decoded media audio and does not require microphone access.

How do popup and Android PiP differ?+

Popup is WizeStream’s floating player. Native PiP is a separate Android 8+ system feature. They have different settings and permissions.

Can I repeat a passage or adjust captions?+

Supported non-live playback offers A–B repeat and session-only subtitle timing. The newer independent A–B enable switch is off by default and postdates 1.41.0. See playback.

Is every video available in 360°?+

No. Genuine spherical content and correct projection information are needed for a meaningful 360° experience. Forcing a flat video into a projection does not create a spherical recording.

Can I rewind every live stream?+

Only as far as the broadcaster’s available DVR history allows. A stream can end, change formats or stop serving historical segments.

Your library

Do subscriptions change my YouTube account?+

No. They are stored locally in WizeStream. You do not need to sign in to maintain a local subscription library.

Why is an upload missing from What’s New?+

Check group, service, content/watch-state, date and blocking filters, then refresh. Feeds are not unlimited historical archives. Use the feed guide.

Can I import a NewPipe backup?+

Compatible backups are supported through Settings → Backup and restore. Keep the original export until you have verified the imported result.

Does FreeTube transfer work in stable 1.41.0?+

The new FreeTube subscription/playlist import and export was merged after that release. Use an appropriate newer build and the migration guide.

Does Device Sync copy video files?+

No. It can transfer supported completed-download metadata. Missing media remains Not local and must be fetched again or copied separately.

Is sync a backup?+

No. Synchronization merges supported records between trusted devices. Keep an independent export for portable recovery.

Are all settings separated by profile?+

No. Profiles separate supported library records, but some settings and data categories remain app-wide. Review the relevant guide and sync controls.

Downloads

Are companion captions embedded in the video?+

No. The supported companion-caption workflow queues a separate sidecar subtitle file with a full video download.

Why did a scheduled download not start exactly on time?+

A download window permits transfers during an interval; it is not an exact alarm. Android scheduling, battery, network and charging constraints can delay work.

Can offline cleanup remove every file on my phone?+

The optional retention workflow is limited to tracked automatic downloads and excludes supported protected, manual/imported and changed-size files. Review its candidate preview.

Privacy

Does Incognito hide my IP address?+

No. It pauses supported activity recording. Services, proxies and network providers can still receive normal connection information.

Are SponsorBlock contributions anonymous?+

No. They are explicit public community actions using a persistent private contribution identity, with normal network connection information. The ID is excluded from normal WizeStream backups and Device Sync.

Why is Translate missing on a comment?+

The feature needs Android 12+, an already-ready on-device language pair, and suitable language detection. WizeStream does not download a model or fall back to cloud translation.

Does this website track documentation searches?+

Search runs in your browser and sends no query to a search server. This site includes no analytics, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. A chosen light/dark preference may be stored locally.

Devices & themes

Does Android Auto show videos?+

No. WizeStream’s Auto integration is audio-oriented. Debug or nightly builds can also be subject to Android Auto’s app-discovery rules.

Why can a cast receiver fail on a video that plays on my phone?+

A receiver can support different codecs or require a compatible source URL. Not every service stream exposes a castable format.

Are theme packs executable plugins?+

No. They are validated data-only JSON packs, with bounded embedded PNG artwork where supported. Scripts and remote assets are outside the supported format.

Does Glass style every dialog?+

No. It applies to supported navigation surfaces and layouts, with opaque fallbacks in some runtime conditions. Static listening layouts are not animated mechanical players.

Help

Where should I report an issue?+

Report a WizeStream problem to WizeStream first, including extractor or service failures. Use Support, include a public example and your app/Android versions, and remove sensitive data from logs.
